Working with Claude

Mark a piece Ready for Claude once its photos and measurements are in. Then ask Claude to “check the shop folder.” Claude reads the tags and photos, researches the piece, and writes the title, 13 tags, attributes, description, and a price range back onto the piece. It then shows as Listing drafted.

Once a month, download Etsy's Sold Orders CSV and monthly statement into the folder's etsy/ subfolder. Claude matches sales to pieces and posts fees.
