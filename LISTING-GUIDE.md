# Listing guide: Etsy and Vinted search for vintage clothing

This is the playbook Claude follows when it writes a piece's `listing`. It was researched in September 2026 from Etsy's Seller Handbook, the eRank and Marmalead sites, and experienced-seller sources. Update it when Etsy changes its guidance.

## How Etsy search decides

- **Matching comes first.** A listing can only show up if the search words match its title, tags, attributes, category, description or first photo. ([How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487))
- **Then ranking.** Matching listings are ranked by relevance (exact phrase matches help), listing quality, customer service, recency and shopper history.
- **Vintage has little sales history to build on.** Every piece is one of a kind, so conversion history rarely builds up. What wins is:
  - exact, specific matching: era, brand, fabric, garment and style;
  - completeness: 13 tags, every attribute filled, the deepest category, and a strong first photo.
- **Recency.** New listings get a small, short boost. Renewing gives an even smaller one.

## Title (Etsy)

- Keep it under 15 words, with no repeated words. This is Etsy's current advice. ([New Guidance for Listing Titles](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697))
- **Formula:** `Decade + Brand (if known) + Color + Material + Style/cut + Item + Size/fit`
- Leave out words like "beautiful," gift phrases, "free shipping," and "vintage" on its own. The vintage attribute already covers that.
- Examples:
  - `70s Western Pearl Snap Shirt, Brown Plaid Cotton Blend, Men's Medium`
  - `90s Levi's 550 Relaxed Tapered Jeans, Light Wash Denim, 32x30`
  - `60s Mod Shift Dress, Orange Geometric Knit, Mini Length, Small`
  - `80s Pendleton Wool Car Coat, Camel Tan, Women's Large`

## Tags (Etsy): all 13, each 20 characters or fewer

- Use **2–4 word phrases people actually type**, e.g. "pearl snap shirt," "high waisted mom jeans," "wool winter coat."
- **Cover the other era spellings.** If the title says "70s," put "1970s" or "seventies" in a tag. It's unclear how far Etsy treats these as the same, so cover both.
- **Aesthetic words, only when true:** western, cowgirl, boho, hippie, mod, grunge, workwear, prairie, cottagecore, Y2K, preppy, minimalist, equestrian, collegiate.
- **Collector details found on the tags:** union made, single stitch, talon zipper, big E, selvedge, deadstock, made in USA.
- **One fit tag,** e.g. "womens medium coat," if the size isn't already in the title.
- A tag may reuse a title word inside a *new* phrase. Don't copy the title or an attribute value word for word.
- **Wasted tags:**
  - single words, or "vintage" alone;
  - colors or materials that are already attributes;
  - subjective words;
  - more than one gift tag.
- Check any new term against Etsy's search-bar suggestions or Marketplace Insights before relying on it.

## Attributes and category (Etsy)

- **Category:** pick the deepest one, e.g. Clothing › Women's › Dresses.
- **Always fill:**
  - Who made it = *another company or person*
  - What is it = *a finished product*
  - When made = the decade (vintage means 20+ years old)
  - Primary and secondary color, material, size, style, occasion, sleeve length, neckline
- **Size attribute = modern equivalent from the measurements, not the tag size.** A 1960s size 10 is roughly a modern 2–4. Put the tag size in the description.
- Only listings that have an attribute show up when a buyer filters by it. ([Add Attributes](https://www.etsy.com/se-en/seller-handbook/article/add-attributes-to-help-increase-your/604203126614))

## Description (Etsy)

1. **First one or two sentences:** a natural restatement of the key terms (era, brand, fabric, style, fit). Etsy reads the start of the description.
2. **Measurements,** in inches, laid flat. Include the shop's measurement note (Settings), the tag size, and an estimated modern size.
3. **Details:** label or brand, fabric content from the care tag, closures, lining, where it was made.
4. **Condition:** a grade (Excellent / Very good / Good / As-is), then every flaw and where it is, pointing to the photo ("pinhole near left cuff, photo 6").
5. Care, and a short shop line. Use short paragraphs or bullets, not one wall of text.

## Photos and other levers

- **First photo:** a clean, full front shot, 2000px or more, with no text on it. Etsy allows up to 20 photos. Show:
  - front, back and details;
  - both the brand and care tags;
  - every flaw.
- A worn or mannequin shot often converts better than flat-lay alone, but that's seller experience, not an Etsy rule.
- **Shipping:** US listings with shipping of $6 or more rank lower, except $35+ items using Etsy's free-shipping guarantee. On pieces $35 and up, build shipping into the price and offer free shipping. ([Shipping price in search](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1293023712519))
- **Shop policies:** use Etsy's policy template, which Etsy says can help.
- **Etsy Ads:** optional. Try a small daily budget and watch whether it pays for itself.

## Vinted

- **Fees:** Vinted charges sellers no fee; the buyer pays a protection fee. Prepaid shipping labels are generated after a sale. So a Vinted price can sit a little under the Etsy price and still net the same or more. ([Vinted US 2026 guide](https://www.telvin-bot.com/en/blog/vinted-us-2026-reseller-guide/))
- **Title:** `Brand + Item + Key detail + Size`, shorter than Etsy's, e.g. `Levi's 550 Relaxed Tapered Jeans 90s Light Wash 32x30`.
- **Fields:** category, brand (or "Vintage"/"Unbranded"), size, color, material, and condition. Condition must be one of: *New with tags*, *New without tags*, *Very good*, *Good*, *Satisfactory*.
- **Description:**
  - what it is;
  - specific condition, with flaws and photo numbers;
  - fit and fabric;
  - measurements;
  - a line of style keywords written as a sentence, then 3–5 hashtags at the end (`#70s #western #pearlsnap`).
  - Vinted searches the title and description text, so write keywords into sentences rather than keyword lists. ([AutoLister](https://autolister.app/blog/how-to-write-vinted-descriptions-that-sell))
- **What sells there:** Vinted US favors everyday prices (roughly $8–$35), and Y2K/vintage is a growth category. Higher-end pieces usually belong on Etsy first.
- **Crosslisting:** fine to list the same piece on both sites. The app flags a piece that has sold on one site and is still live on the other.

## Pricing

- **Comps:** eBay *sold* listings, plus current Etsy asks for the same maker, era and garment. Note condition differences.
- **Record:** a `suggested` price, a `low`–`high` range and a `vinted` price, with a one-line `note` on what the comps showed.
- **Round to how shops price,** e.g. $38 or $48, not $41.37.

## Tools

- **Free:**
  - Etsy Marketplace Insights: 15 searches a week, real Etsy volumes, beta. ([Marketplace Insights](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1404564905677))
  - Etsy search-bar suggestions.
  - Shop Stats → search terms.
  - eRank's free plan: 5 keyword lookups a day. ([eRank plans](https://erank.com/plans))
- **Marmalead:** about $19/month. It offers keyword volume, engagement and seasonality, but independent reviewers question how accurate its volumes are. Worth a 14-day trial alongside eRank before paying for either. ([Marmalead pricing](https://marmalead.com/pricing/), [review](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/the-og-etsy-seo-tool-tested-marmalead-review))
- **Best free signal for this shop:** her own Etsy Stats search terms. Claude can read them in the desktop app and fold the winners back into this guide.
