## Voice
- Friendly and plainspoken, like a knowledgeable friend describing a find. Describe what's special about the piece (the fabric, the cut, the label) without superlatives.
- Short paragraphs. Honest, specific condition notes.
- A closing shop line is fine when it fits her voice, e.g. "Bundle and save: I offer discounts on multiple items. Follow @serendipityvintageco for sales."
