# Bookkeeping and taxes

The goal is records that keep themselves: capture each number once, when it happens, and reconcile once a month. This is organizing support, not tax advice. Their tax preparer makes the calls.

## What gets tracked
- **Hauls (cost of goods).** One per receipt: date, store, receipt total, number of pieces bought for resale, and round-trip miles. The total is split evenly across the pieces, unless one piece was clearly the prize and gets more. Each piece gets a code (YYMM-###, e.g. 2609-014) written on masking tape, so its cost follows it to the sale.
- **Sales.** Etsy comes from the Sold Orders CSV. Vinted and consignment come from their payout records or statements. Record sale price, date, where it sold, fees or the consignment shop's cut, and any shipping label paid.
- **Etsy fees** (listing, transaction, processing, Etsy Ads) come from the monthly statement. Post them as monthly expenses, not per piece.
- **Mileage** for sourcing trips, at the IRS business standard rate: 72.5¢/mile for 2026 ([irs.gov](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-sets-2026-business-standard-mileage-rate-at-725-cents-per-mile-up-25-cents)). Count the drive once per trip, even when a trip covers several stores.
- **Other expenses:**
  - cleaning and repair supplies
  - shipping supplies and mailers
  - postage
  - equipment (steamer, rack, lighting)
  - consignment fees
  - advertising
- **Inventory at year end:** pieces bought but not sold by Dec 31, at cost. These carry into next year rather than being deducted now; confirm the method with the preparer.

## Low-effort capture
- **At the store:** one photo of the receipt lying on the pile of finds. That records the store, date, total and items in a single shot.
- **Sourcing card:** use one card only for sourcing, so the bank statement is a backup record.
- **Missing receipts:** a quick note (date, store, total, pieces) made the same day counts as a record.
- **Once a month (~15 minutes):** download the Etsy Sold Orders CSV and monthly statement, add any consignment statements, and ask Claude to reconcile. Claude flags sales with no purchase cost, hauls with no receipt photo, and deposits that don't match.
- **Tax money:** set aside a fixed percentage of each payout.

## Year-end summary for the preparer
- **Income:** gross sales by platform.
- **Costs of selling:** fees and consignment cuts, and shipping labels.
- **Cost of goods sold,** and **purchases during the year.**
- **Expenses** by category.
- **Mileage:** business miles × rate.
- **Ending inventory** at cost.
- **Estimated net profit.**
- **Backup:** the full ledger of pieces, hauls and expenses, with receipt photos.

## Notes
- Etsy may or may not issue a 1099-K, depending on volume. The income is reportable either way, so keep her own records rather than relying on Etsy's forms.
- Questions for the preparer:
  - expensing inventory when bought vs. when sold (cost of goods)
  - home-office use
  - standard mileage vs. actual vehicle expenses
